About the Study

What is this research about?

This study aims to develop an effective, easy-to-follow programme to help adults futureproof themselves against cardiovascular disease by reducing cholesterol levels and improving their cardiovascular health.


We will do this by using a dedicated smartphone application to support participants in adhering to a Mediterranean diet and/or a resistance exercise regimen. The study forms part of a PhD degree programme at Liverpool John Moores University.


⚠️
Participation is entirely voluntary

You are under no obligation to take part in this study. If you choose to participate, you are free to withdraw at any time and for any reason without penalty or consequences of any kind. Your decision will be fully respected.

Who can take part?

Eligibility Criteria

Please read the criteria below carefully before expressing interest. You must meet all inclusion criteria and none of the exclusion criteria.

✔ You should apply if…

You meet all of the following conditions

  • You are aged between 30 and 55 years
  • You own and regularly use a smartphone

✖ You must NOT apply if…

You are ineligible if any of the following apply

  • You currently use any cardiometabolic medication, particularly lipid-lowering drugs or supplements that may affect LDL cholesterol levels
  • You are a woman who is pregnant or nursing
  • You have a chronic health condition (e.g. cancer, cardiovascular disease, diabetes, etc.)
  • You have a history of cardiovascular disease or a major adverse cardiovascular event (e.g. stroke or heart attack)
  • You have a known food allergy or intolerance
  • You are currently following a restrictive eating pattern (e.g. vegan, vegetarian, carnivore, or ketogenic diet)
  • You have participated in regular resistance exercise within the last 12 months
Study Design

The Four Study Groups

If you take part, you will be randomly allocated to one of these four groups.

Group 1
🏋️

Resistance Exercise

You will follow a structured 3-day-per-week resistance exercise programme designed by the research team.

Group 2
🥗

Mediterranean Diet

You will follow a Mediterranean-style diet with guidance and support provided through the study application.

Group 3
🏋️🥗

Combination

You will follow both the Mediterranean-style diet and the 3-day-per-week resistance exercise programme.

Group 4
📋

Control Group

You will receive general advice on healthy eating and exercise patterns without a structured programme.

What's involved?

Assessments & Measurements

All participants will undergo the following assessments at the beginning and end of the study period. These will be carried out at LJMU facilities by trained researchers.

🩺

Blood Pressure Test

Standard resting blood pressure measurement using a validated device.

📏

Anthropometric Measurements

Weight, height, waist circumference, BMI, and body composition assessment.

✊

Grip Strength Test

A handgrip dynamometer will be used to assess muscular strength.

🧬

Advanced Blood Tests

Comprehensive blood panel including cholesterol (LDL, HDL, total) and related markers.

❤️

Cardiovascular Tests

Flow mediated dilation (FMD) and pulse wave velocity (PWV) to assess vascular health.

📝

Mediterranean Diet Score

A validated questionnaire to assess adherence to the Mediterranean dietary pattern.
