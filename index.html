About the Study

What is this research about?

Merseyside Exercise and Diet intervention for Aggregation of LDL (MEDAL): a randomised controlled trial investigating the effects of diet and exercise on cardiovascular health.


This study is investigating whether a Mediterranean-style diet and/or resistance exercise, followed for four weeks, can improve cholesterol and other markers of cardiovascular health in healthy adults.


Participants in the diet and exercise groups use a dedicated smartphone app to support them. The study forms part of a PhD degree programme at Liverpool John Moores University and has been approved by the LJMU Research Ethics Committee (ref: 260629LJMUREC694).

Who can take part?

Eligibility Criteria

To take part, you must meet all of the inclusion criteria and none of the exclusion criteria. Eligibility is confirmed through a short online screening questionnaire and a health and exercise readiness questionnaire (PAR-Q).

✔ You may be able to take part if…

You meet all of the following conditions

  • You are aged between 30 and 55 years
  • You own and regularly use a smartphone

✖ You cannot take part if…

You are ineligible if any of the following apply

  • You currently use any cardiometabolic medication, particularly lipid-lowering drugs or supplements that may affect LDL cholesterol levels
  • You are a woman who is pregnant or nursing
  • You have a chronic health condition (e.g. cancer, cardiovascular disease, diabetes, etc.)
  • You have a history of cardiovascular disease or a major adverse cardiovascular event (e.g. stroke or heart attack)
  • You have a known food allergy or intolerance
  • You are currently following a restrictive eating pattern (e.g. vegan, vegetarian, carnivore, or ketogenic diet)
  • You have participated in regular resistance exercise within the last 12 months
Study Design

The Four Study Groups

If you take part, you will be randomly allocated (like flipping a coin) to one of these four groups for four weeks. You cannot choose your group.

Group 1
🏋️

Resistance Exercise

You will follow a structured 3-day-per-week resistance exercise programme designed by the research team.

Group 2
🥗

Mediterranean Diet

You will follow a Mediterranean-style diet with guidance and support provided through the study application.

Group 3
🏋️🥗

Combination

You will follow both the Mediterranean-style diet and the 3-day-per-week resistance exercise programme.

Group 4
📋

Control Group

You will carry on with your usual diet and activity for four weeks. You will not receive a programme, advice or the study app during the study; this group lets us compare the other groups against normal day-to-day life. At the end, you will be offered a booklet with the diet and exercise guidance.

What's involved?

Assessments & Measurements

All participants complete the following assessments at the start and end of the four weeks, at the Tom Reilly Building (LJMU Byrom Street Campus), carried out by trained researchers. Before each visit you will be asked to fast for 8 hours (water only) and avoid caffeine, alcohol and vigorous exercise for 24 hours.

🩺

Blood Pressure Test

Standard resting blood pressure measurement using a validated device.

📏

Anthropometric Measurements

Weight, height, waist circumference, BMI, and body composition assessment.

✊

Grip Strength Test

A handgrip dynamometer will be used to assess muscular strength.

🧬

Blood Sample

A small fasting blood sample (no more than 15 mL, about one tablespoon) to measure cholesterol, blood glucose and related markers.

❤️

Blood Vessel Function (FMD)

Flow-mediated dilation: an ultrasound of the artery in your upper arm while a cuff on your forearm is inflated for 5 minutes, to see how well the artery widens.

📝

Diet and Exercise Questionnaires

A short 14-item Mediterranean diet questionnaire and a muscle-strengthening exercise questionnaire, completed during each visit. Before each visit you will also keep a 3-day food diary.

Taking part

What happens if I'm interested?

You can stop at any step. Registering interest does not commit you to taking part.

  1. Read the Participant Information Sheet

    It explains the study in full, including possible risks. Read the Participant Information Sheet (PDF)

  2. Register interest and complete the screening questions

    A short online form (LJMU Jisc Online Surveys) asks you to confirm you have read the information sheet, answer a few eligibility questions and give your name and email address.

  3. We contact you

    The researcher will email you, answer any questions and let you know whether you are eligible. You will have at least 24 hours to decide.

  4. Give consent by email

    If you decide to take part, you confirm the consent statements by email, then keep a 3-day food diary before your first visit.

  5. Visit 1 at LJMU (about 2 hours)

    Health assessments, then random allocation to one of the four groups.

  6. Four weeks in your group, then Visit 2 (about 2 hours)

    The same assessments are repeated, plus a short exit questionnaire.

Why take part?

Why take part?

The main benefit is helping to advance research into how diet and exercise affect heart health. There is no payment for taking part. At the end of the study, you will receive a summary of your own measurements. These are for your information only and are not a clinical or personal health assessment; if we find something unexpected, we may advise you to seek appropriate advice, if you agree to this on the consent form.
