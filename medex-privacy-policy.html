1

What MedEx is

MedEx is the app built for the MEDAL Trial — LJMU's research study looking at how a Mediterranean diet and resistance exercise can help prevent cardiovascular disease. If you're reading this, you're either taking part in the study or considering it, and MedEx is how you'll log your diet and exercise as part of your participation.

MedEx isn't available to the public. It's only ever handed out to people who've agreed to take part in the MEDAL Trial. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app collects about you and how that information is looked after.

2

What we collect

MedEx only collects what it needs to work for you. Here's the full list:

  • Study ID & PINA participant number and PIN you're given at enrolment, used to keep your entries separate from everyone else's. MedEx doesn't ask for your name, date of birth, or email address.
  • Diet logsThe food and drink you record each day and week — entered by you, whenever you use the app.
  • Exercise logsThe sets, reps, and weights you log during your workouts.
  • Notification tokenA code your phone generates so MedEx can send you reminders. On its own, it doesn't identify you.
MedEx never touches your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location — and there's no advertising software of any kind built into the app.
3

How your information is used

Everything MedEx collects exists to support the MEDAL Trial. In practice, that means:

  • For youLetting you track your own diet and exercise adherence over the course of the study, and see your progress.
  • For the research teamHelping the team monitor how the study is going, in aggregate, across all participants.
  • RemindersSending you a gentle nudge if you haven't logged in a while.

Your information is never sold, and it's never used for marketing or advertising, by us or anyone else.

4

Storage & security

Your data is stored with Supabase, a secure database provider, and is encrypted while it's sent between your phone and the database. Only members of the MEDAL Trial research team at LJMU can access identifiable study data — no one else.

5

How long we keep it

Your data stays with us for the duration of the MEDAL Trial. After the study wraps up, it's retained, archived, or anonymised in line with LJMU's research data management policy, and with what you agreed to in your Participant Information Sheet and Consent Form.

If you decide to withdraw from the study, get in touch with the research team using the contact details on your Participant Information Sheet — they'll talk you through what happens to your data next.

6

Your rights

As a participant, your data protection rights are set out in the consent you gave when joining the MEDAL Trial — see your Participant Information Sheet and Consent Form for the full detail. If you have questions about your data, or want to exercise any of your rights, reach out to the research team or LJMU's Data Protection Officer using the details in that documentation.

7

Children's privacy

MedEx is built for adult participants in the MEDAL Trial only. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

8

Changes to this policy

If we make any material changes to this policy, the research team will let active participants know directly.

9

Questions about your data?

The research team is happy to help with anything on this page.
